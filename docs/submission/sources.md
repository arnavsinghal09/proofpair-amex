# Source list

Use sources narrowly. They establish the dispute workflow and investigation obligations; they do not validate ProofPair’s synthetic outcomes.

1. [American Express — U.S. Disputes Reference Guide](https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/merchant/pdf/manage-disputes/US-Disputes-Reference-Guide.pdf)  
   Primary reference for dispute/chargeback reason guidance and merchant response documentation.
2. [American Express — Disputes Support Center for Merchants](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/merchant/support-center/disputes.html)  
   Supports the operating need for a central case view, supporting-document upload, urgent-case prioritization and status tracking.
3. [American Express — India disputes webinar / Compelling Evidence Policy](https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-in/business/merchant/pdf/india_disputes_webinar.pdf)  
   Supports the relevance of weighing merchant evidence for goods/services not received while preserving issuer review.
4. [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Regulation Z §1026.13 and official interpretation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/interp-13/)  
   Supports reasonable investigation, documentary explanation, and careful treatment of missing consumer cooperation.
5. [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Regulation Z §1026.12](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/2023-05-15/12/)  
   The official interpretation describes an independent assessment using cardholder and merchant information where possible.

## Citation discipline

- Do not present the prototype’s five reason packs as complete AMEX policy coverage.
- Do not claim the display weights or 30-point thresholds are American Express policy; they are explicit prototype configuration.
- Do not claim a regulatory compliance determination.
- Do not use unsourced market-size, accuracy, cost-savings or cycle-time numbers.
